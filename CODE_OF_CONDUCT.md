# Code of conduct

misomoon follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in issues and every other project space.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and give feedback on the work, not the
person. Harassment, insults, and personal attacks are not accepted.

To report a problem, use
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/jackzard/misomoon/security/advisories/new)
with "Conduct" in the title, or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are kept
confidential. The maintainers decide on the response, as the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines describe.
