# Quickstart

Three steps take you from nothing to your first visit. It takes about two minutes.

## 1. Get misomoon

Pick one:

- **misomoon Cloud.** Sign up at [app.misomoon.com/?signup](https://app.misomoon.com/?signup).
  The first 7 days are free, with no card ([misomoon Cloud](cloud.md)).
- **Your own Cloudflare account.** Deploy with one button, then open your instance and
  create the first account ([Self-host on Cloudflare](self-hosting.md)).

## 2. Add your site

Add your site's domain. The dashboard then shows your script tag:

```html
<script defer src="https://analytics.example.com/m.js?s=your-site"></script>
```

Paste it into the `<head>` of every page. The [install guides](integrations.md) show
where it goes on WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Next.js, and other platforms.

## 3. See your first visit

Open your site in a browser. You appear in **Live** within a few seconds, and the setup
guide on **Overview** turns green. If you don't, select **Check my site** on the guide to
see whether the tag is on your home page, then see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
